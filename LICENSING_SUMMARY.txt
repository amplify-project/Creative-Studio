AMPLIFY AUDIO MODELS - LICENSING SUMMARY
Date: 2 November 2026 (distortion detector replaced; first version 5 October 2026)

Scope: the two classifier models and the pretrained embedding model one of
them depends on:

  content_mlp.onnx    - content classifier (singing / speech / instrumental /
                        other), runs on YAMNet embeddings
  distortion/distortion_detector.onnx
                      - distortion detector (clean / clipping / bass boost /
                        low fidelity), self-contained: its own encoder plus
                        DSP measurements. Built by Salsa Sound, 2026-10-30.
  yamnet_model.onnx   - pretrained audio embedding model (Google)

The previous distortion pair (dac_encoder.onnx, the Descript Audio Codec
encoder, plus distortion_mlp.onnx) is no longer used or distributed.

This is an engineering summary, not legal advice.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. SUMMARY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- The pretrained model (YAMNet) has a permissive licence (Apache 2.0). It
  can be shipped commercially if the licence notice is included.

- The distortion detector was trained only on audio licensed for commercial
  use (public domain, CC0, CC BY). Its encoder was distilled from the DAC
  encoder (MIT) — trained to reproduce DAC's outputs, DAC used as teacher
  only — and contains no DAC weights. Its attribution notice is
  distortion/NOTICE.txt and must ship with it.

- content_mlp was trained partly on two datasets that are licensed for
  non-commercial use only: ESC-50 and UrbanSound8K (both CC BY-NC 3.0),
  about 6% of its training data. It is legally unsettled whether a model
  trained on non-commercial data is itself restricted to non-commercial use.
  The cautious position is that the current content_mlp build should not be
  used commercially until it is retrained without these datasets or a
  commercial licence is obtained. This no longer applies to distortion
  detection.

               Section 4, two levels upstream.

  Our own model. Its status depends on the training data in Sections 3

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. PLANNED NEXT STEPS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Distortion detection: done (2026-11-02). The detector was retrained on
commercially licensed audio only and ships with distortion/NOTICE.txt.

Content classification, for commercial use, either:

  (a) retrain content_mlp without ESC-50 and UrbanSound8K, replacing them
      with commercially usable sources such as the CC BY ESC-10 subset, the
      CC0 and CC BY clips of FSD50K, and the existing MUSAN noise; or

  (b) obtain commercial licences from the ESC-50 and UrbanSound8K authors.

Ship an attribution/NOTICE file containing: YAMNet (Apache 2.0), ONNX
Runtime (MIT, if bundled), MUSAN per-file credits, VocalSet (CC BY 4.0) and
SingVERSE (CC BY 4.0), alongside distortion/NOTICE.txt.

Open item: the singing training examples in content_mlp came from an
earlier project that applied reverb and layering augmentation. We are
checking whether that augmentation introduced any other third-party audio.
