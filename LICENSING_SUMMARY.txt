AMPLIFY AUDIO MODELS - LICENSING SUMMARY
Date: 5 October 2026

Scope: the two classifier models and the two pretrained embedding models they
depend on:

  content_mlp.onnx    - content classifier (singing / speech / instrumental /
                        other), runs on YAMNet embeddings
  distortion_mlp.onnx - distortion classifier (clipping / bandwidth limit /
                        bass boost / codec artefacts), runs on DAC encoder
                        embeddings plus 8 hand-written DSP features
  yamnet_model.onnx   - pretrained audio embedding model (Google)
  dac_encoder.onnx    - pretrained audio encoder (Descript Audio Codec, 16 kHz)

This is an engineering summary, not legal advice.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. SUMMARY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- The two pretrained models (YAMNet and the DAC encoder) have permissive
  licences (Apache 2.0 and MIT). They can be shipped commercially if the
  licence notices are included.

- Most of the training data is public domain, CC0 or CC BY. It can be used
  commercially with attribution.

- Both classifiers were trained partly on two datasets that are licensed for
  non-commercial use only: ESC-50 and UrbanSound8K (both CC BY-NC 3.0).
  These make up about 6% of the content_mlp training data and about 19% of
  the distortion_mlp training data. It is legally unsettled whether a model
  trained on non-commercial data is itself restricted to non-commercial use.
  The cautious position is that the current builds should not be used
  commercially until they are retrained without these datasets or a
  commercial licence is obtained.

models. In both models they supply environmental and urban sounds: the
"other" class in content_mlp, and clean and distorted examples in

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. PLANNED NEXT STEPS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

For commercial use, either:

  (a) retrain both classifiers without ESC-50 and UrbanSound8K, replacing
      them with commercially usable sources such as the CC BY ESC-10
      subset, the CC0 and CC BY clips of FSD50K, and the existing MUSAN
      noise; or

  (b) obtain commercial licences from the ESC-50 and UrbanSound8K authors.

Ship an attribution/NOTICE file containing: YAMNet (Apache 2.0), DAC (MIT),
ONNX Runtime (MIT, if bundled), MUSAN per-file credits, VocalSet (CC BY 4.0)
and SingVERSE (CC BY 4.0).

Open item: the singing training examples in content_mlp came from an
earlier project that applied reverb and layering augmentation. We are
checking whether that augmentation introduced any other third-party audio.
